BUSINESS ANALYSIS

Questions the project answers

What changed?

Revenue and profit performance are measured over time and across business dimensions.

Why did it change?

Revenue is decomposed using Quantity × Revenue/Unit and Transactions × AOV.

Where is the profit?

Products are classified by revenue and gross margin to identify strategic priorities.

What happens next?

Trend, moving-average and forecast analysis provide a forward-looking view.
