# Business Case

## Executive Problem

The business needs a reliable way to understand sales performance beyond headline revenue.

Management requires visibility into:

- Revenue and profit performance
- Product and brand contribution
- Margin quality
- Geographic performance
- Payment behavior
- Revenue drivers
- Volatility and outliers
- Future revenue direction
- Pricing and volume decisions

## Objective

Build a decision-support solution that connects Power BI reporting with Excel-based statistical and scenario analysis.

## Analytical Questions

### Descriptive
What happened?

### Diagnostic
Why did it happen?

### Predictive
What may happen next?

### Prescriptive
What should management do?

## Expected Business Outcomes

The project should enable management to:

- Identify high-value products and brands
- Protect high-margin revenue
- Diagnose margin leakage
- Understand volume versus value drivers
- Monitor revenue concentration
- Identify unusual transactions
- Evaluate future sales direction
- Test commercial scenarios before implementation

## Success Criteria

The project is successful when a stakeholder can move from a KPI to:

1. The driver
2. The risk/opportunity
3. The expected future direction
4. A quantified action

## Stakeholder Narrative

The presentation should avoid leading with formulas.

Use:

**Performance → Drivers → Risk → Forecast → Scenarios → Actions**
